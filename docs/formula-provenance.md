# Formula provenance

`provenance/sources.yaml` records each external source, revision, access date, license state, and source hash. `LICENSES/README.md` records the unresolved license boundary.

The repository contains no Pine source. It does not claim formula equivalence with a TradingView indicator.

All source-derived plugins remain deferred. Promotion requires pinned expected-output vectors and an independent conforming implementation.

A promotion review must preserve exact Decimal and UTC time behavior. It must also preserve completed-candle, no-lookahead, warm-up, and timeframe contracts.

Do not infer formulas from screenshots or prose. Do not publish source-derived behavior before the license and conformance gates close.
